## Executive Summary

This framework provides a structured approach to identifying, analyzing, and managing cybersecurity risks within your organization.

### 1.1 Risk Identification

- Threat landscape analysis

- Impact analysis

### 1.2 Risk Analysis
- Likelihood assessment
- Impact evaluation
- Risk scoring matrix
- Risk prioritization

### 1.3 Risk Evaluation
- Risk tolerance definition

### 4.1 Technical Vulnerabilities
- Unpatched systems
- Weak configurations
- Outdated software
- Insecure protocols
- Missing security controls

### 4.2 Process Vulnerabilities
- Inadequate policies
- Poor change management
- Insufficient training
- Weak access controls

### 4.3 Human Vulnerabilities

- Security awareness levels
- Compliance adherence

## 5. Risk Scoring Matrix

| Likelihood | Very Low (1) | Low (2) | Medium (3) | High (4) | Very High (5) |
|------------|--------------|---------|------------|----------|---------------|
| **Very High (5)** | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| **High (4)** | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| **Medium (3)** | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| **Low (2)** | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| **Very Low (1)** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

### Risk Levels:
- **Critical (20-25):** Immediate action required
- **High (15-19):** Action required within 30 days
- **Medium (8-14):** Action required within 90 days
- **Low (4-7):** Monitor and review
- **Very Low (1-3):** Accept risk

## 6. Risk Treatment Options

### 6.1 Risk Mitigation
- Implement security controls
- Enhance monitoring
- Improve processes
- Provide training

### 6.2 Risk Transfer
- Cyber insurance
- Outsourcing
- Contractual agreements
- Third-party services

### 6.3 Risk Avoidance
- Eliminate risky activities
- Change business processes
- Remove vulnerable systems
- Discontinue services

### 6.4 Risk Acceptance
- Document decision rationale
- Monitor risk levels
- Regular review schedule
- Contingency planning
